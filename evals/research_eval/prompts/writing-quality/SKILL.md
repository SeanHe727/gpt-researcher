# Writing quality

This group judges the report itself, apart from its citations. Prompts in
this folder include shared sections with `@include writing-quality#<section>`;
the text under a `## <section>` heading is sent to the judge as it is.

No shared sections yet: they are written when this group is reviewed in
stage 2, so its prompts are judged again only once. Candidates: the
full / partial / none coverage scale (comprehensiveness.md), the 0-10 score
anchors (quality_score.md) and what counts as an opinion (toxicity_bias.md).
